1. Scope of Services

Consultant will provide professional services consisting of operations optimization and technology efficiency consulting, delivered remotely via web conference, phone, and email. No on-site travel is included unless separately agreed in writing. Services are organized as follows:

Group 1 – Operations Optimization

  • Process Inefficiencies Analysis
  • Process Assessment & Analysis
  • Efficiency Improvement Roadmaps
  • Gap Analysis & Opportunity Identification
  • Solution Evaluation & Priority Assessment
  • Workflow Streamlining
  • Process Refinement

Group 2 – Technology Efficiency

  • Technology Inefficiencies Analysis
  • Technology Stack Assessment and Analysis
  • Technology improvement roadmaps & education plans
  • Solution Evaluation & Technology Selection
  • ROI Analysis & Implementation Planning
  • System Integrations, Automations, and AI assistants
  • Technology Platform Improvements

Services for platforms, tools, or technologies outside the areas described above may be provided on a best-effort basis as agreed by the parties.

2. Service Plan, Rate, and Payment Terms

Consultant’s rate for Services under this Agreement is $150.00 (one hundred fifty dollars) per hour. Client will be invoiced at the end of each month for Services performed during the prior month. Each invoice will describe the Services provided, hours worked, and the amount due.

3. Service Hours and Response Time

Services will be provided Monday through Friday between the hours of 9:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time, except for holidays. Consultant will respond to Client requests by the next business day.

4. Term and Termination

The initial term of this Agreement is month to month, beginning on the Effective Date. This Agreement will automatically renew at the end of each calendar month for an additional one (1) month unless either party provides written notice of termination.

Termination by Client. Client may avoid automatic renewal and terminate this Agreement by providing written notice at the beginning of the last week of the then-current month. Upon such notice, the Agreement will end at the conclusion of that month.

Termination for breach. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice for a material breach, provided that the terminating party has given the other party at least fourteen (14) days’ written notice of the breach and an opportunity to cure. Termination for breach does not affect any other remedies available to the terminating party.

5. Terms in This Agreement Prevail

In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and any other agreement between the parties, the terms of this Agreement will control with respect to the Services and subject matter described herein.
