- Physical street address of your main business location

### ✅ Mailing Address (If Different)

- If different from principal office address
- Can be a P.O. Box

**Options:**
1. **General Purpose** (Recommended for flexibility):

   - "Any lawful business activity"

### ✅ Duration

**Options:**
1. **Perpetual** (Recommended - most common)
   - LLC continues indefinitely

### ✅ Management Structure

**Two types of LLC management:**

1. **Member-Managed** (Most Common for Small Businesses)

## Filing Instructions

### Step 1: Access Michigan's Online Filing System

**Direct Link:** https://www.michigan.gov/corporations/file-online

**Alternative:** Go to https://www.michigan.gov/corporations → Click "File Online"

### Step 2: Create Account or Log In

- If first time: Create account (free)
- If returning: Log in with existing credentials
- Account allows you to:
  - Save and continue later
  - Track filing status
  - Receive confirmation

### Step 3: Select "Form LLC"

- Choose "Articles of Organization - Limited Liability Company"
- Click "Start New Filing" or similar button

### Step 4: Enter Business Information

Follow the online form and enter:

1. **Business Name:**
   - Enter: "Fresh Roots Consulting, LLC"
   - System will check availability as you type
   - If available, proceed

2. **Registered Agent:**
   - Name: [Your name or service name]
   - Address: [Physical Michigan address]
   - Phone: [Contact number]

3. **Principal Office Address:**
   - Street address
   - City, State, ZIP
   - (Can be same as registered agent)

4. **Mailing Address:**
   - If different from principal office
   - Can be P.O. Box

5. **Business Purpose:**
   - Select or enter: "Any lawful purpose"
   - Or specific purpose if preferred

6. **Duration:**
   - Select: "Perpetual"

7. **Management:**
   - Select: "Member-Managed"
   - (Or "Manager-Managed" if applicable)

8. **Organizer Information:**
   - Your name and address

### Step 5: Review and Submit

- Review all information carefully
- Check for typos (especially address)
- Submit payment ($50 for online filing)

### Step 6: Payment

- Online filing: $50 (credit/debit card)
- Processing: Usually immediate to 1-2 business days
- You'll receive confirmation email

### Step 7: Receive Confirmation

- Save your confirmation/certificate
- Keep this document safe - you'll need it for:
  - Opening bank account
  - Obtaining EIN
  - Other business filings

## Paper Filing (Alternative)

If you prefer to file by mail:

1. **Download Form:**
   - https://www.michigan.gov/corporations/forms
   - Form: "Articles of Organization for Limited Liability Company"

2. **Complete Form:**
   - Fill out manually or type
   - Follow same information requirements above

3. **Mail with Payment:**
   - Fee: $60 (paper filing)
   - Make check payable to "State of Michigan"
   - Mail to address on form
   - Processing: 5-7 business days

## After Filing - Next Steps

Once you receive confirmation that your LLC is formed:

### Immediate Next Steps:

1. **Create Operating Agreement** (Highly Recommended)
   - Not required by state, but strongly recommended
   - Defines ownership, management, and operational procedures
   - Helps maintain liability protection

2. **Obtain EIN (Employer Identification Number)**
   - Apply at: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-ein-online
   - Free and immediate online
   - Needed for bank accounts and taxes

3. **Open Business Bank Account**
   - Take Articles of Organization
   - Take EIN
   - Take Operating Agreement (if created)
   - Never mix personal and business finances

4. **Register for State Taxes** (If Required)
   - Sales tax (if selling taxable goods/services)
   - Withholding tax (if hiring employees)
   - https://www.michigan.gov/taxes

### Within First Month:

5. **Get Business Insurance**
   - General liability
   - Professional liability/E&O (important for consultants)

6. **File Name Reservation** (If you did this first, no action needed)

## Important Notes

⚠️ **Before Filing:**
- Double-check name availability one more time
- Verify addresses are correct (hard to change later)
- Have payment method ready

⚠️ **After Filing:**
- Keep all documents in a safe place
- Don't lose your confirmation/certificate
- File Annual Report by February 15 each year ($25 fee)

⚠️ **Common Mistakes to Avoid:**
- Using P.O. Box for registered agent address
- Typos in business name or addresses
- Not having payment ready
- Forgetting to create Operating Agreement after filing
